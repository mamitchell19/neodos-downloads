NEW IN 3.9: USB Wi-Fi sticks
  * Wi-Fi for PCs whose built-in card NeoDOS cannot drive: USB Wi-Fi sticks with the MediaTek MT7601U chip (many
    of the cheapest "150 Mbps" sticks, e.g. ASUS USB-N10 Nano B1, D-Link DWA-127 rev B, Edimax EW-7711MAC, and
    unbranded ones listed as "MT7601"). Plug the stick in (USB RESCAN if NeoDOS is already running), then use the
    same commands as for a built-in card: WIFI SCAN, WIFI CONNECT "name" "password", WIFI DISCONNECT.
    Open and WPA2 (AES) networks work; the stick sends at 24 Mbit/s (802.11g), so expect about 1-2 MB/s.
    Not yet tried with a real stick: it was tested against a software model of the stick and of access points.
    When a laptop has both a supported built-in card and a stick, NeoDOS uses the built-in card.
  * Through the stick, NeoDOS also answers when the access point renews its group key (every hour on many routers),
    so the connection keeps receiving broadcasts after that.

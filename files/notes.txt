NEW IN 3.10: faster, steadier USB Wi-Fi
  * The MT7601U stick now joins 802.11n networks (one stream, 20 MHz, up to 65 Mbit/s) with QoS, and picks its
    sending speed by itself: it tries faster rates now and then and stays at the one that gets the most through
    (WIFI shows the current rate, e.g. "sending at 52 (MCS 5) Mbit/s"). Networks without 802.11n use 6-54 Mbit/s.
  * It keeps its radio in trim while it runs, as the chip maker intends: receiver gain from the access point's
    signal, baseband settings and calibration as the stick warms up or cools down, the crystal frequency against the
    access point's, and - on sticks built for it - closed-loop control of the transmit power. WIFI shows the stick's
    temperature change.
  * Packets the access point bundles together (A-MSDU) are taken apart; block-acknowledgement requests are declined
    politely, so the access point simply sends single frames.
  * Still tested only against a software model of the stick and of access points, not a real stick.

NEW IN 3.7: a tidier stick in Windows
  * Windows used to give the NeoDOS stick three drive letters and offer to format two of them (small start-up
    areas it cannot read - formatting one could stop the stick from starting). Now it shows only NEOSETTINGS.
  * If NeoDOS ever stops with a "system halted" screen on a live stick, the crash report is now also saved as
    CRASH.TXT on the NEOSETTINGS drive, so it can be opened and sent from Windows.

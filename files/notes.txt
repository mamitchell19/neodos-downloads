NEW IN 3.6: wired network on modern laptops
  * The network port built into most laptops and office PCs since 2013 (Intel I217, I218 and I219 - e.g. ThinkPad
    P51 and P52) now works when Windows or the PC's management engine left it asleep: NeoDOS wakes it the way
    Intel's own drivers do, switches it to full power and waits long enough (up to 7 seconds) for the cable to
    come up. "IPCONFIG /ALL" shows the adapter's details if there is still no connection - a photo of them helps
    to find out why.
  * NeoDOS USB Creator 1.2 writes sticks on Windows 11 too (1.1 stopped at 4 MB with error 38).
